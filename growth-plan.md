# Growth Plan: 500 Registrations in 7 Days (₹2,000)

## Slide 1: The Student
- **Who:** Final-year B.Tech students (CSE/IT/AI-ML first, then ECE/EEE) in tier-2 and tier-3 colleges, entering placement season.
- **Pain:** Their resumes show only academic projects. They hear "learn AI" everywhere but have built nothing, and they assume it needs advanced math, a GPU, or paid courses.
- **Why they care:** Recruiters ask "what have you built?" and they have no strong answer.

## Slide 2: The Offer
- **Hook:** "Build your first AI project in 60 minutes and leave with a live link for your resume."
- **What makes them register:** free, no AI experience needed, certificate for attendees, project starter kit for those who invite friends, and social proof ("your classmates already registered").

## Slide 3: Channels and Budget
| Channel | Target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Campus ambassadors | 250 | Recruit ~25 final-years via LinkedIn/WhatsApp DMs. Each gets a unique referral code and a ready-made message to share in class and branch groups. Goal: 10 sign-ups each. |
| Clubs & communities | 150 | Approach ~10 clubs (GDSC, IEEE, CSI, coding clubs). Offer a club leaderboard and a shout-out. Goal: 15 each. |
| Referral loop | 100 | The thank-you page says "Invite 2 friends, unlock the project starter kit." |

**Why it works:** students trust peers and campus groups more than ads, and ambassadors and clubs bring existing WhatsApp groups with built-in reach. The 250/150/100 split is a planning assumption, not a forecast. I would check it daily.

**Budget (₹2,000):**
- ₹900: rewards for top 3 ambassadors (₹500 / ₹250 / ₹150 vouchers)
- ₹600: small Instagram test to compare two messages (not a core channel)
- ₹500: buffer
- ₹0: tools (Canva, Google Forms/Sheets, Netlify free tiers)

## Slide 4: 7-Day Plan
- **Day 1:** Set up the registration page, referral codes, WhatsApp copy, and tracker.
- **Days 2-3:** Recruit ambassadors, approach clubs, launch the first posts.
- **Days 4-5:** Share a sample project preview, send referral reminders, check channel numbers.
- **Day 6:** Urgency message ("seats filling") and re-engage interested students.
- **Day 7:** Final reminder, then report the best-performing channels.

## Slide 5: Measure and Optimize
- **Daily KPIs:** total registrations, progress toward 500, registrations by source, registrations per referral code.
- **Rules:** move effort to the channel with the most sign-ups, test a new message where sign-ups are low, and give extra support to the top ambassadors.
- **Limits of the prototype:** the dashboard uses illustrative sample data and browser-local entries. It does not track visits, clicks, or attendance, so it shows registration share, not conversion rate.
